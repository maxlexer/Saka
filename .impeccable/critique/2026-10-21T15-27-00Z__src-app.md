## Design specificity
Hero, intro, card-to-modal glide and the modal are authored for SAKA. Below the fold it falls back to a generic minimal theme: eyebrow plus heading on every section (several restate each other), 20 identical tall tiles on /products, no material cue after the hero. Detector: CLI clean (0 findings with config); browser flags were brand-mandated (cream-palette, overused-font Montserrat), intentional hidden layers (buried-raster on the dark floor and sprite), its own overlay (text-occlusion), plus kicker-above-heading on every page and a borderline all-caps-body on card titles.
